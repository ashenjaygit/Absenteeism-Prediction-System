# eArrow AI Engineer Intern: Interview Preparation & Demo Guide

## 1. Project Title & Executive Summary
**Project:** AI-Powered Employee Absenteeism Prediction & Intelligent HR Decision Support System  
**Domain:** Apparel Manufacturing Workforce Operations (MAS Context) / Enterprise HR & Payroll (eArrow Target Use Case)

### The Elevator Pitch (30–45 Seconds)
> *"In apparel manufacturing and enterprise operations, unscheduled employee absenteeism disrupts assembly lines, creates bottlenecks, and inflates overtime labor costs. I developed a 4-layer AI Decision Support System:*
> 1. * **Predictive Layer:** Uses Gradient Boosting (94% accuracy) to classify individual absenteeism risk and an LSTM/XGBoost hybrid model for daily absence forecasting.*
> 2. * **Explainable AI (XAI) Layer:** Applies SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) to decompose predictions into mathematical feature contributions, eliminating black-box opacity.*
> 3. * **Generative AI Layer:** Powered by Groq, translating raw SHAP weights into empathetic, non-punitive HR recommendations and synthesizing executive management reports.*
> 4. * **Domain RAG Layer:** Employs ChromaDB to retrieve factory SOPs and Sri Lankan labor regulations (Shop & Office Employees Act / Factories Ordinance), answering HR policy questions with verified citations.*
> *This transforms raw predictions into an actionable decision-support platform tailored for enterprise HR suites like eArrow."*

## 4. Key Interview Questions & High-Scoring Answers

### Q1: "Why did you use SHAP instead of default feature importance?"
**Answer:**
> *"Global feature importance only tells us how important a feature was across the entire training dataset on average. But HR managers don't manage averages—they manage individual employees. SHAP calculates local Shapley values based on cooperative game theory, quantifying the exact positive or negative contribution of each feature for a single specific employee's prediction."*

### Q2: "Why choose Groq over running a local model or standard OpenAI API?"
**Answer:**
> *"Enterprise HR applications require near real-time interaction without latency lag. Groq's LPU (Language Processing Unit) architecture delivers 300–500 tokens per second for high-capacity open models, enabling instant report generation and chat responsiveness. For sensitive HR environments, it can also seamlessly switch to private or on-premise open-weight models."*

### Q3: "How does this prevent hallucination in HR policy answers?"
**Answer:**
> *"We implemented a strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture. Rather than relying on the LLM's parametric memory, the user's query is converted into an embedding and searched against verified apparel HR SOPs in ChromaDB. The prompt explicitly instructs the LLM to only answer based on retrieved context and cite the exact policy clauses."*

### Q4: "How does this align with eArrow's enterprise solutions?"
**Answer:**
> *"eArrow builds enterprise HR and Payroll software. While payroll systems capture retrospective attendance data, adding predictive and generative decision support creates proactive business value: forecasting production shortfalls, mitigating employee burnout, and empowering HR managers with natural language insights directly inside the HR workflow."*
