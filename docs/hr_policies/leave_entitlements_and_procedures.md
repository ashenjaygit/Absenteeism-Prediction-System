# Employee Leave Entitlements & Application Procedures

## 1. Statutory Leave Entitlements (Sri Lankan Labor & Factory Regulations)
In alignment with the Shop and Office Employees Act, the Factories Ordinance, and company collective bargaining frameworks, permanent employees receive the following annual leave allocations:

1. **Annual Leave (14 Days)**:
   - Earned based on the preceding year's service completion.
   - Intended for planned rest and family commitments.
   - Applications must be submitted via the HR Self-Service Portal at least **7 working days prior** to the intended leave date.
   - A maximum of 7 consecutive days can be taken in a single block without specialized General Manager HR approval.

2. **Casual / Sick Leave (7 to 21 Days)**:
   - Permitted for medical ailments and unforeseen family obligations.
   - For absences exceeding **two (2) consecutive days**, a valid Medical Certificate issued by a Registered Medical Practitioner (MBBS/Govt. Ayurvedic Practitioner) or the factory Occupational Health Center (OHC) medical officer must be submitted upon resuming work.
   - Sick leave cannot be claimed retrospectively after return without verified medical documentation.

3. **Maternity Leave**:
   - 84 working days (12 weeks) paid maternity leave for female employees, conforming to Sri Lankan national standards.
   - Pre-natal checkup leave of 2 half-days per trimester with certified medical clinic cards.

4. **Paternity Leave**:
   - 5 to 7 working days paid paternity leave for male employees upon childbirth, redeemable within 30 days of delivery.

## 2. Leave Exhaustion & Unpaid Leave
- When accrued leave balances (Annual, Sick, Maternity/Paternity) reach zero (0), further absences are treated as Leave Without Pay (LWOP).
- LWOP requires explicit recommendation by the Head of Department (HOD) and approval by the Human Resources Director.
