# Apparel Manufacturing Attendance & Shift Regularity Policy

## 1. Scope & Objective
This policy governs workforce attendance, punctuality, and shift adherence across all apparel manufacturing lines, sewing clusters, cutting, quality assurance, and finishing units. Regular attendance is critical to line-balancing, Standard Allowed Minute (SAM) efficiency, and on-time shipment commitments.

## 2. Shift Schedules & Punctuality
- **Day Shift (Shift A)**: 07:00 AM – 03:30 PM (including 30-minute meal break and two 10-minute rest intervals).
- **Night / Extended Shift (Shift B)**: 03:30 PM – 12:00 AM (applicable in designated export delivery cycles).
- **Grace Period**: A 10-minute biometric grace window is permitted. Fingerprint / facial recognition scans after 07:10 AM are logged as "Late Attendance".
- **Late Arrivals**: Accumulating three (3) late attendance instances within a calendar month incurs a deduction of a half-day casual leave or an HR counseling session.

## 3. Shift Swapping & Roster Changes
- Production operators wishing to exchange scheduled shifts must submit a **Shift Transfer Request Form** signed by both Section Supervisors at least **48 hours in advance**.
- Unapproved shift abandonment or swapping constitutes a procedural violation.

## 4. Line Balancing & Absence Notification Protocol
- If an operator is unable to report for duty due to acute illness or emergency, they must notify the **Section Group Leader / HR Front Desk by 06:30 AM** (at least 30 minutes before shift commencement).
- Early notification enables Section Supervisors to assign buffer operators or multi-skilled cross-trained tailors to maintain continuous conveyor line pacing.
