# Unauthorized Absence Management & Disciplinary Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 1. Definition of Unauthorized Absence
An absence is classified as **"Unauthorized Leave" (Unscheduled / Absent Without Official Leave - AWOL)** when an employee fails to report for their rostered shift without:
1. Prior approved leave in the HR Portal; or
2. Notice communicated to the Section Leader / HR Helpline within 4 hours of shift start; or
3. Legitimate documentary proof (e.g., sudden hospitalization, catastrophic emergency) delivered within 48 hours.

## 2. Progressive Escalation Protocol
To maintain ethical standards and fair labor practices, HR applies progressive steps rather than immediate punitive termination:

- **Stage 1 (1 to 2 Days Unauthorized)**:
  - Line Supervisor and HR Executive conduct a supportive return-to-work check-in.
  - Verification of employee safety and domestic situation.
  - Informal counseling record maintained; absence logged as Leave Without Pay.

- **Stage 2 (3 to 5 Consecutive Days or Frequent Habitual Absenteeism)**:
  - Formal Written Advisory Letter issued by HR Employee Relations.
  - Mandatory meeting with factory Welfare & Counselor to explore underlying stressors (e.g., transport hardship, elder care, chronic health issues).
  - Agreement on a 30-day attendance rehabilitation plan.

- **Stage 3 (More than 5 Consecutive Days AWOL without Notice)**:
  - Registered notice sent to the employee's residential address registered on payroll.
  - Opportunity provided to show cause within seven (7) working days.
  - If no communication or justifiable defense is provided, formal inquiry under standard labor tribunal guidelines commences.

## 3. Decision-Support Principle for HR Managers
- Predictive ML risk indicators and high absenteeism predictions **must not be used as disciplinary proof**.
- Predictions serve exclusively as early-warning indicators to enable proactive engagement, counseling, and operational contingency planning before unauthorized leave occurs.
